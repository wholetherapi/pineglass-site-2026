Island microgrids need sub-seasonal irradiance forecasts to schedule diesel top-ups.
Sundial Forecast combines satellite-derived clear-sky indices with a gradient-boosted
residual model and reports calibrated prediction intervals at one to six week horizons.

Results on twelve island sites are summarised in the symposium submission.  The released
artifact contains the reference implementation, the feature pipeline and the backtest harness.
