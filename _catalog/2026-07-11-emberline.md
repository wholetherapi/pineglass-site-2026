Emberline converts raw VIIRS thermal detections into hourly wildfire perimeter estimates
without manual digitisation: density clustering, concave hull construction, day-over-day
identity tracking and growth and spread vectors, validated against official incident
perimeters.

The released artifact bundles the perimeter builder, the thermal detection filters and the
validation notebooks used in the letter.
